# Problem Framing and Analytical Review

صياغة المشكلات التحليلية ومراجعة التحليلات

Data Science Track · SDAIA Academy · DS-310 · Level 300

Turn vague requests into answerable decision questions, plan the analysis, and review analyses by people and assistants by consequence

Participants practise the work before and after an analysis, using requests from Ghadeer Water Company, a fictional water utility. They turn vague requests into decision questions with an estimand, a metric and a decision rule, interview a role-played manager, use domain knowledge of metering, billing and repair to see where the data mislead, and test whether data and deadline make the question answerable. They then review analyses by peers and by an assistant and write review notes ranked by consequence. Metric definition is DS-307; team review standards are DS-407.

**Format:** Intensive: 3 teaching days of four sessions and a capstone day, with guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task

**Course page:** https://sdaiaacademy.github.io/ds-310/

## Case study

**Ghadeer Water Company.** A fictional water utility with six districts whose managers send vague requests about water
loss, leak repair, billing and complaints. Participants frame those requests with an estimand and a decision rule,
interview a role-played operations manager and metering engineer, map how meter reads become bills, stress-test the
water-loss and customer-service metric tree, write and attack analysis plans, and review three seeded-flaw analyses,
one written by the assistant Sareea. The capstone moves to a second case, the Building Permits Department of Hima
Municipality and its permits backlog. All data is synthetic, generated by `labs/prepare_data.py` with a fixed seed.

**AI policy.** AI-off in Lab 1.1 and in the supervised framing check. AI-tutor in the other labs: the tutor asks
questions about a draft and never supplies a framing (a free hosted model when a key is set, a rule-based stand-in
otherwise). AI-full in the capstone, where an assistant-generated analysis is also an object of review.

**Responsible use.** Every framing card records who is affected and whether personal data are needed at all. The
feasibility lab applies data classification, data minimisation and the sharing timeframe for data held by another
entity. Assistant-generated analyses are rerun and verified before any number is used, and labelled as
assistant-generated.

## Capstone

A framing card, analysis plan and peer review for a second synthetic case (a municipal permits backlog), submitted as a repository and presented while the role-played sponsor changes a constraint, with ten minutes of defence

**Submission:** a link to a public GitHub repository holding the framing card, the analysis plan (two pages at
most) and the review notes on the participant's own variant of the supplied analysis, ranked by consequence, with the
capstone README (`labs/capstone/README.md`) filled in. The capstone is individual. The brief and its synthetic data are in `labs/capstone/`. The talk lasts eight minutes; during
it the sponsor changes one constraint, and ten minutes of defence follow.

**Assessment.** A supervised framing check without AI assistance (three requests framed in 40 minutes, 20%), the
capstone framing card, plan and review notes on the rubric (50%) and the live defence with an unseen change of
constraint (30%). Passing: 70% overall and at least 60% on the live defence. Seeded flaws differ between participants
and review notes are matched against them.
